## A familiar design, reimagined

Material 3 is a custom theme for **Better Lyrics**, bringing Google's Material 3 design language to YouTube Music.

The idea was simple: create an interface that feels modern, expressive, and comfortable to use without getting in the way of the music. Inspired by Material You, the theme combines a dark interface with vibrant accents, rounded typography, and carefully considered visual details.

Rather than overwhelming the interface with unnecessary elements, the design keeps the focus where it belongs: on the music and its lyrics.

## Designed around the details

Every element is designed to feel cohesive with the Material 3 aesthetic.

- **Expressive dark interface:** A dark foundation complemented by colorful accents.
- **Google Sans Flex (Rounded):** Rounded typography for a softer, more approachable appearance.
- **Dynamic color styling:** A Material You-inspired approach to colors and visual hierarchy.
- **Expressive loading indicator:** Replaces the default spinner with a custom Material 3 Expressive loading animation.
- **Minimal layout:** Keeps lyrics readable and the overall experience uncluttered.
