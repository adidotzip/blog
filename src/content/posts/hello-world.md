A little space to collect thoughts, document things I'm learning, and share projects as they take shape.

More soon. ✨

## Writing here

Posts are just Markdown files. Add a new `.md` file to `src/content/posts/`, give it a title, description, and publication date in the frontmatter, and it will appear on the home page automatically.
